The Memory Burn

Allow your candle to burn for at least 2 to 3 hours on its first lighting until the wax melt pool reaches all outer glass edges. This locks the memory and eliminates tunneling permanently.

02

Trim to 1/4 Inch

Always pinch off charred wood tips or trim cotton wicks to 5mm prior to every single burn session. A neat wick ensures a steady flame, maximum fragrance throw, and clean soot-free glass.

03

Safe Sanctuary

Burn on heat-resistant surfaces away from drafts, pets, and children. Extinguish after 4 consecutive hours using a metal snuffer or lid to preserve the delicate aroma and prevent smoldering.
