Our Sacred Vow

Artisan Craftsmanship & Sacred Purity

We reject the industrial assembly lines of commercial room mists and mass-market paraffin wax. True aromatic artistry respects slow time, honest provenance, and botanical non-negotiables.

1 Ethical Sourcing & Cold-Pressed Absolutes expand_more
Direct partnerships with smallholder family farms across Kannauj for wild rose extracts and Kerala for sustainable sandalwood plantations certified with zero deforestation.
2 Lead-Free Braided Cotton Wicks expand_more
Braided natural cotton and paper filaments customized strictly to vessel diameter, preventing black soot, tunneling, or zinc oxide vapors in living spaces.
3 Hand-Poured in Temperature-Controlled Suites expand_more
Poured at precisely 57°C into pre-warmed stone vessels to prevent surface crystallization and ensure a perfectly level burn horizon from the very first light.
